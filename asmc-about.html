About ASMC

A home for every Adamjee student who wants music to be part of their school life — not just an extracurricular line on a form.

Our mission

The ACPS Music Club gives Adamjee students a regular, structured space to sing, play, and perform — regardless of how much experience they walk in with. We believe the best musicians are made through consistent practice and real stages, not just talent.

The club runs its own rehearsals, hosts school performances, and puts forward its flagship group — the Adamjee School Band — for bigger stages and inter-school competitions.

What members gain

  • Weekly, structured rehearsal time with peers and section leads
  • Real performance experience at assemblies, concerts, and competitions
  • A path into the Adamjee School Band for members who want to go further
  • Basic music theory and ensemble skills, taught alongside practice
  • A club community that carries across grades and years

How it started

The Music Club began as a handful of students practicing together after school. As interest grew across grades, it became a proper club with regular rehearsals and its own performing groups — including the Adamjee School Band, formed in 2025 by members who wanted to take their sound further.
