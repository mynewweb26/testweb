About the Club

A home for every Adamjee student who wants the arts to be part of school life — not just an extracurricular line on a form.

Our mission

The ACPS Cultural Club gives students a regular, welcoming space to perform, create and celebrate — whatever their experience when they walk in.

The club plans school programmes and festivals, supports student artists, and includes ASMC, the Music Club, which has its own band and committee.

What members gain

  • Real stages at school programmes, festivals and competitions
  • Hands-on experience planning and running events
  • Room to try music, drama, dance, art and writing
  • Friendships and teamwork across grades
  • A route into ASMC and the Adamjee School Band

How it started

The Cultural Club was founded in 2024 so that students had a dedicated place for the arts beyond the classroom. As interest grew, it began running school programmes and gave rise to ASMC, the Music Club, and the Adamjee School Band.
