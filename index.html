What we do

Open to every student who wants to take part in the cultural life of the school.

Stage & performance

Music, drama, dance and recitation — regular chances to perform for the school and for families.

Art & creativity

Drawing, craft, design and exhibitions that put students' own work on display.

Celebrations

National days, Pohela Boishakh and school festivals, planned and run by students.
